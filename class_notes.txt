Dear all,

I hope you are doing well.

Please find attached the grading scheme for the VIP course. The final grade will be composed of the following components:

Lab Notes (10%)
Every week, you are required to submit lab notes summarizing your progress during the in-person session. This may include algorithms implemented, experiments conducted, ablation studies, and any errors or problems encountered.
A template will be shared with you and must be followed. All lab notes should be uploaded to a shared Excel sheet that will be provided. 

Machine Learning Models (30%)
You are required to implement machine learning models as part of the course project. This component accounts for 30% of the final grade, with 7.5% per model, meaning that at least four models must be implemented per student.
For each model:

2.5% is awarded for a correct and functional implementation,

5% is awarded for code quality, including adherence to the shared abstract class, documentation, modularity, clarity, and comments.

Dataset Collection or Investment Strategies (10%)
In the second part of the VIP, each student will choose one of the following tracks:

Dataset collection, or

Investment strategies implementation.

This component accounts for 10% of the final grade.

For dataset collection, you are required to:

Write clear documentation describing the collected data and sources,

Detail the data web scraping pipeline,

Specify any proprietary or commercial-use restrictions, if applicable,

Process and clean the data with proper documentation,

Perform an exploratory data analysis (EDA).

For investment strategies, you are required to:

Conduct a literature review (papers, blog posts, lecture notes),

Summarize the main ideas and the reasoning behind each investment strategy,

Highlight the advantages and limitations of each strategy,

Implement the strategies in clean, well-documented code following the shared abstract class.

Midterm Presentation (10%)
You will give a midterm presentation (date to be announced). This presentation accounts for 10% of your final grade. The goal is to share your progress with your peers, explain the implemented models, and report preliminary results.

Final Report (40%)
You are required to submit a final report summarizing the overall findings of the VIP. Each member is responsible for writing the sections related to their own contributions, including:

Models implemented,

Assumptions,

Advantages and limitations,

Results obtained.

This report accounts for 40% of the final grade.

Attendance Policy
Any unjustified absence will result in a 5-point penalty applied to the final grade.

Credits Policy

VIP members registered for 0 credits must obtain a minimum final grade of 70% to pass the course.

VIP members registered for credit will receive a letter grade following the FRE grading scheme.

Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions.

encounter errors you cannot solve, or

the code/model developed during the week (one model per week),

any questions, difficulties, or issues you encountered.
